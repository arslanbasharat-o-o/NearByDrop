# Code of Conduct

Nearby Drop welcomes respectful bug reports, questions, and suggestions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and patient with other people.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy. Do not post personal information, private files, credentials, or pairing codes.
- Respect the project’s contribution rules and license.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to this repository’s issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces managed by the maintainer.

Report concerns privately to **arslanbasharat414@gmail.com**. Include links and relevant context; do not post sensitive evidence publicly. Reports will be handled as privately as practical, and retaliation against someone reporting in good faith is prohibited.

The maintainer may remove content, close or lock conversations, warn participants, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation based on severity and repeated behavior. Participants may request reconsideration through the same private contact.

Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
