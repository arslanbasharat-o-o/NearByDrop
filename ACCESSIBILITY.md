# Accessibility

Nearby Drop aims to make its Mac app and website usable with assistive technology. Accessibility feedback is welcome and helps prioritize improvements.

## Supported environments and current features

The Mac app requires macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon or Intel. It uses native SwiftUI controls and includes accessibility labels for controls such as settings, downloads, USB navigation, and file actions. Some animations respond to the macOS Reduce Motion preference.

The website includes a skip-to-content link, labeled theme controls, light and dark appearances, and reduced-motion handling for its decorative hero effect. Native disclosure controls reveal additional details.

## Known limitations

A complete end-to-end VoiceOver and keyboard audit of every app workflow has not been completed. These features do not constitute a claim of full accessibility or WCAG conformance. QR scanning, device discovery, and transfer workflows may present barriers that need further testing with assistive technology.

## Report an accessibility barrier

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/arslanbasharat-o-o/NearByDrop/issues/new?template=bug_report.md), or email **arslanbasharat414@gmail.com** if the report contains private information.

Please include the app version or website URL, operating system and browser if relevant, assistive technology and version, steps to reproduce, and the expected behavior. Share only redacted screenshots or logs. The maintainer will review reports as capacity allows; no fixed response or resolution time is promised.
