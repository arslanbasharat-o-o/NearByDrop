// 01 ARCHITECTURE

Peer-to-Peer Transfer Pipeline

Zero central coordination. Transfers run exclusively between your phone and your Mac across your local network interface.

[ 01 / DISCOVERY ]

mDNS & Bluetooth

Nearby Drop advertises the Google Nearby Share service name (_FC9F5ED42C8A._tcp.local.) over local multicast DNS. Your Mac immediately shows up in Android's native Quick Share sheet.

TRANSPORT: IPv4 / IPv6 Multicast
[ 02 / HANDSHAKE ]

UKEY2 Negotiation

A mutual cryptographic handshake is established using Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman (P-256). Both devices compute an identical 6-digit confirmation authentication PIN before any file bytes can be transmitted.

CRYPTO: ECDH P-256 + HKDF SHA-256
[ 03 / STREAMING ]

Direct Socket Stream

Payload frames stream directly through an encrypted local TCP socket. Transfer speed is bounded strictly by your Wi-Fi router's bandwidth and your device storage controller—not arbitrary cloud rate limits.

FRAMING: Protobuf Payload Packets
[ 04 / PERSISTENCE ]

Finder Delivery

Transferred files are directly committed into your user ~/Downloads/ directory. An alert with a Finder reveal button notifies you once the payload is flushed to disk.

PATH: ~/Downloads/ (Configurable)

[ WHAT NEARBY DROP ACTUALLY DOES ]
  • ✓ Receives files from Android Quick Share directly on your Mac.
  • ✓ Runs entirely offline over local Wi-Fi, hotspot, or direct USB-C.
  • ✓ Verifies connection with cryptographic UKEY2 ECDH matching PIN.
  • ✓ Supports USB-C MTP storage browsing via integrated libmtp.
  • ✓ 100% Free and open source with zero telemetry or tracking.
[ CURRENT SYSTEM SCOPE & BOUNDARIES ]
  • ! Receiver Mode First: Optimized primarily to receive transfers sent from Android to macOS.

  • ! Wi-Fi Isolation: Public guest Wi-Fi networks that block peer-to-peer traffic (client isolation) will require using a mobile hotspot or USB cable.

Frequently Answered Questions

Yes. Nearby Drop communicates directly over your local Wi-Fi router or phone mobile hotspot using direct TCP sockets. It requires zero outside internet connectivity. If you are on an airplane or remote location, turning on your phone's personal hotspot and connecting your Mac will work immediately.
We explicitly reject inflated marketing claims. Actual speeds are determined by your Wi-Fi router frequency (5GHz vs 2.4GHz), channel interference, and device storage speed. On modern 5GHz Wi-Fi (Wi-Fi 5 or Wi-Fi 6), expect typical real-world transfer rates between 25 MB/s and 80 MB/s. Over direct USB-C cable using the MTP manager, transfers run at high hardware speeds (~80–150 MB/s).
Nearby Drop is a 100% free community open-source project. We do not charge money or pay Apple's $99/year Developer Program subscription for an official Developer ID certificate.
