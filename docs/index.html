Quick Share for Mac

Move files between your Mac and Android.

Nearby Drop is a menu bar app for sending and receiving over Quick Share, QR code, or USB.

Download for Mac ↗ See how it works

Free download · macOS 14+ · Apple silicon and Intel

A few simple steps

Open. Choose. Share.

Nearby Drop keeps each step in one menu bar window.

  1. 01

    Open Nearby Drop

    Find it in your Mac’s menu bar whenever you need it.

  2. 02

    Choose a direction

    Select files to send, or get your Mac ready to receive.

  3. 03

    Connect and share

    Use Quick Share, scan a QR code, or plug in over USB.

From your Mac to your phone

Pick a file. Pick a device.

Choose files on your Mac and send them to a nearby device. When it’s more convenient, share with a QR code instead.

Designed for nearby sharing

No cloud relay.

Wireless transfers happen over your local network. Nearby Drop doesn’t relay files through a cloud service. USB transfers use the cable.
