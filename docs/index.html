Three-Second Workflow

How It Works

1

Tap Share on Android

Open any photo, video, or document on your phone, tap the standard system Share button, and select Quick Share.

2

Select Your Mac

Your Mac will instantly appear in the discovery sheet. With our dynamic IPv4 mDNS broadcaster, pairing is instantaneous.

3

Accept & Done

The menu bar dropdown pops open with a chime. Confirm the matching PIN code or toggle "Trust Device" to automatically receive files in the future.
