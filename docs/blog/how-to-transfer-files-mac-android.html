How to Transfer Files from Android to Mac with Quick Share

AUTHOR: ARSLAN BASHARAT • PUBLISHED OCTOBER 22, 2026 • PROTOCOL: GOOGLE QUICK SHARE WIRE

How do I transfer files from Android to Mac?

For wireless transfer, put the Mac and Android phone on a reachable local network, open Nearby Drop on the Mac, then select files on Android and choose the Mac in Quick Share. Confirm the request in Nearby Drop. If network discovery is blocked, connect by USB and use MTP.

Android-to-Mac file transfer has more than one Quick Share route. On supported phones, Google's native Quick Share can send to a Mac through AirDrop. Google's compatibility guide currently lists Pixel 8a or later and select models from Samsung, Oppo, OnePlus, Vivo, Xiaomi, Honor, Transsion, and Motorola; support varies by model. Nearby Drop is a separate Mac app for local-network Quick Share transfers and USB MTP storage browsing.

[ NATIVE OPTION ]

Use native Quick Share-to-AirDrop when your phone supports it

This route does not require Nearby Drop. On your Mac, set AirDrop visibility to Everyone for 10 minutes. On Android, select the files, tap Share → Quick Share, choose the Mac, then accept the AirDrop notification.

If your phone does not support native AirDrop interoperability, Google provides a Quick Share QR-code fallback for Mac. It requires internet access; Google says files are encrypted and available to download for 24 hours. Nearby Drop instead transfers over the reachable local network, or through a USB cable with MTP.

This guide covers wireless Quick Share on a reachable local network and a direct USB MTP connection. Transfer speed depends on the devices and network or cable. Android-generated QR receiving on Mac passed with a Pixel 8 Pro during v2.3.2 validation and is included in the current release.

Nearby Drop is free to download and distributed under a proprietary license. It is independent and is not affiliated with Google or Apple.

[ METHOD 01 ]

Wireless transfer with Quick Share

On an Android device that supports Quick Share, select files and look for Nearby Drop in the device list while the Mac app is running. The Mac and phone need a reachable local network; discovery can vary with router and hotspot settings.

STEP 1: INSTALL NEARBY DROP ON YOUR MAC

STEP 2: CONNECT TO A REACHABLE LOCAL WI-FI NETWORK

Connect the Mac and phone to the same local Wi-Fi network and allow local network access when macOS asks. LTE-only phone-hotspot discovery has not been verified and may not show the Mac; use a shared Wi-Fi network or USB MTP if discovery fails.

STEP 3: SELECT FILES ON ANDROID AND TAP QUICK SHARE
  1. Open your phone's Gallery or Files app and select the photos, 4K videos, or folders you wish to send.
  2. Tap the Share button and choose Quick Share.
  3. If Nearby Drop appears in the device list, select it. If it does not, check the connection steps below.
STEP 4: ACCEPT THE TRANSFER ON YOUR MAC

Nearby Drop shows the incoming transfer request and matching confirmation PIN. Review it and click Accept. Received files are saved to your Downloads folder. Actual speed depends on the devices and network.

[ TROUBLESHOOTING ]

If your Mac does not appear in Quick Share

  1. Make sure Nearby Drop is open and both devices can reach each other on the same local network. Guest Wi-Fi and client isolation can block discovery.
  2. On the Mac, check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and allow Nearby Drop.
  3. If the network still blocks discovery, use USB MTP. Phone-hotspot discovery varies; LTE-only hotspot discovery is not verified.

See the full connection troubleshooting guide for more checks.

[ QR STATUS ]

Android-to-Mac QR receiving

Android-to-Mac QR receiving passed in a physical Pixel 8 Pro test during v2.3.2 validation, and the fix is included in the current release. The ordinary Quick Share device list and USB MTP are also available. Nearby Drop's separate Mac-generated QR send flow is intended for Mac-to-Android sharing.

[ METHOD 02 ]

Direct USB-C Cable (Hardware MTP Storage Manager)

USB MTP is an alternative when wireless discovery is unavailable or you prefer a cable:

  1. Connect your phone to your Mac using a USB-C to USB-C data cable.
  2. On your phone, unlock the screen and set the USB notification mode to File Transfer / MTP.
  3. Open Nearby Drop's USB tab to browse phone storage and copy files to Finder. Speed depends on the phone, cable, and storage.
FILE TRANSFERS STAY ON THE LOCAL CONNECTION

Wireless transfers use the UKEY2 pairing handshake and a local network connection; USB transfers use the cable. Nearby Drop does not use a cloud relay for file contents. Review the repository's privacy and security notes for implementation details.
